# Quick Start: Generating Weekly Reports

## TL;DR - 3 Easy Steps

1. **Log Your Data**
   - Add some expenses
   - Log meals 
   - Create habits

2. **Go to Reports**
   - Dashboard → AI Intelligence → Weekly Reports
   - Or Direct URL: `http://localhost:5000/ai/reports`

3. **Generate Report**
   - Click **"Generate New Report"** button
   - Wait for it to complete (10-30 seconds)
   - View your personalized insights!

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Make Sure You Have Data
The system works best with some data to analyze. You need at least ONE of:

**Financial Data**:
- Go to Dashboard → Finance → Log Expense
- Add a few expenses (amount, category, date)

**Health Data**:
- Go to Dashboard → Health → Log Meal
- Add meal details (name, calories, protein)

**Habits**:
- Go to Dashboard → Health → Manage Habits
- Create a new habit (name, frequency)
- Complete it a few times

### Step 2: Navigate to Reports Page

**Option A - From Dashboard**:
1. Click on your Dashboard (home page)
2. Look for **AI Intelligence** card on the right
3. Click **Weekly Reports**

**Option B - Direct URL**:
1. Type in address bar: `http://localhost:5000/ai/reports`
2. Press Enter

**Option C - From Navigation**:
1. If there's an AI menu, click on it
2. Select "Weekly Reports"

### Step 3: Generate Your First Report

Once you're on the Reports page:

1. **See Your Data Status**
   - The page shows how much data you have
   - Green badges = You have data for that category
   - Yellow badges = Add more data for better insights

2. **Click "Generate New Report"**
   - Green button in the top right
   - Or if it's your first, click "Generate Your First Report" button

3. **Wait for Generation**
   - You'll see a loading spinner
   - Progress bar indicates generation progress
   - Status shows "Generating..."

4. **Report Complete!**
   - You'll see a success message
   - Page automatically redirects to your new report
   - OR you'll see it added to the list

### Step 4: View Your Report

**From List**:
1. Find your report in the list
2. Click **"View Full Report"**

**Full Report Shows**:
- AI Summary - Personalized insights and recommendations
- Financial Summary - Spending analysis
- Health Summary - Nutrition and habit data
- Habit Analysis - Individual habit performance
- Expense Prediction - Forecasted spending
- Report Info - Generation date and week covered

---

## What Each Section Means

### Financial Summary
- **Total Spent**: Sum of all expenses this month
- **Daily Average**: How much you spend per day on average
- **Top Categories**: Where you're spending the most
- **Trend**: Is your spending increasing or stable?

### Health Summary
- **Average Calories**: Daily calorie intake
- **Average Protein**: Daily protein intake
- **Active Habits**: How many habits you're tracking
- **Consistency**: Percentage of days you completed habits

### Habit Analysis
Shows for each habit:
- **Completion Rate**: % of days completed
- **Current Streak**: How many days in a row
- **Status**: Excellent/Good/Fair/Needs Work

### AI Recommendations
Three actionable suggestions personalized to YOUR data

### Expense Prediction
- **Predicted Daily**: How much you'll likely spend tomorrow
- **Based On**: How many days of historical data
- **Confidence**: How reliable the prediction is

## What If I Have Problems?

### "No reports generated yet"
**This is normal!** 
- Click "Generate Your First Report" button
- Or make sure you have some data logged

### "Error generating report"
**Try these**:
1. Refresh the page
2. Make sure you logged some data (expenses, meals, or habits)
3. Try again in a few moments
4. Check your internet connection

### Report takes very long
**Normal behavior**:
- First report might take 10-30 seconds
- This is because it's calling AI to generate insights
- Just wait, don't refresh the page

### Report shows limited data
**Why**: You might not have much data yet
- Keep logging expenses, meals, and habits
- Generate another report next week
- Reports get better with more data

## Pro Tips

### Best Practices
- **Generate Weekly**: Every Sunday is perfect
- **Log Consistently**: Daily logging = better insights
- **Review Recommendations**: Read the AI suggestions carefully
- **Track Progress**: Compare week-to-week reports

### What Makes Good Data
- **Expenses**: Daily or near-daily logging
- **Meals**: At least 1-2 per day
- **Habits**: Consistent completion

### How to Improve Your Reports
1. Log more expenses (for financial insights)
2. Log meals with accurate calories/protein (for health insights)
3. Create and complete habits daily (for habit insights)
4. Be consistent for at least 1-2 weeks

## Common Questions

**Q: How often should I generate reports?**
A: Once a week is ideal. Generate on the same day each week to track trends.

**Q: Can I generate multiple reports the same day?**
A: Yes, but they'll be very similar. Best to generate fresh weekly.

**Q: What happens if I don't have much data?**
A: Reports still generate, but with limited insights. Add more data over time.

**Q: Can I edit or delete reports?**
A: Yes. On the Reports list, there's a trash icon to delete reports.

**Q: Are my reports private?**
A: Yes. Only you can see your reports.

**Q: How far back do reports look?**
A: 
- Expenses: Last 30 days
- Meals: Last 7 days  
- Habits: All time (but counts recent activity more)

**Q: What's the AI using to generate reports?**
A: Google Gemini AI - reads your data and creates personalized recommendations.

**Q: Can I export my report?**
A: Not yet, but you can screenshot it or take notes.
